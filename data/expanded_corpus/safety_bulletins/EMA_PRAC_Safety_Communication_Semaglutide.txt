## 1. Executive Safety Advisory & Issue Summary
Document ID: DOC-SB-EMA-002 | Organization: European Medicines Agency (EMA)
Title: EMA PRAC Safety Advisory: Diabetic Retinopathy Complications Assessment for Semaglutide
Affected Medicine: Semaglutide
EMA PRAC completed signal review on diabetic retinopathy events observed in SUSTAIN-6 (3.0% vs 1.8%, HR = 1.76). Rapid initial HbA1c reduction in patients with baseline retinopathy increases risk.

--- Page 2 ---
## 2. Pharmacovigilance Risk Evaluation & Signal Analysis
Post-marketing surveillance data, EudraVigilance database queries, and spontaneous MedWatch event filings revealed adverse signal patterns requiring updated guidance.

--- Page 3 ---
## 3. Recommended Clinical Actions for Healthcare Providers
Perform baseline ophthalmological evaluation prior to semaglutide escalation in diabetic patients with established retinopathy.
