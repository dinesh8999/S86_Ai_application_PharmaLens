## 1. Executive Safety Advisory & Issue Summary
Document ID: DOC-SB-FDA-003 | Organization: FDA MedWatch
Title: FDA Safety Alert: Boxed Warning and REMS Protocol for ARIA-E and ARIA-H with Donanemab
Affected Medicine: Donanemab
FDA mandates Boxed Warning detailing risk of Amyloid-Related Imaging Abnormalities (ARIA-E edema 24%, ARIA-H microhemorrhage 31.4%) associated with anti-amyloid therapy.

--- Page 2 ---
## 2. Pharmacovigilance Risk Evaluation & Signal Analysis
Post-marketing surveillance data, EudraVigilance database queries, and spontaneous MedWatch event filings revealed adverse signal patterns requiring updated guidance.

--- Page 3 ---
## 3. Recommended Clinical Actions for Healthcare Providers
Mandatory baseline brain MRI and genetic ApoE testing prior to initiating treatment. Follow MRI monitoring schedule prior to 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 7th infusions.
