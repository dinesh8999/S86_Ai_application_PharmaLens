PHARMACOVIGILANCE SAFETY BULLETIN: DRUG X
Section: Contraindications and Drug Interactions
Drug X is contraindicated in patients with severe hepatic impairment or known hypersensitivity to active ingredients. Concomitant administration with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors may increase systemic exposure to Drug X and requires dose reduction. Renal monitoring is recommended for elderly patients over 75 years of age.
